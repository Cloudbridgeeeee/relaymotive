How a RelayMotive trial runs

  1. Upload service records, week 1

    Send job cards, invoices and fault-code exports for up to 50 vehicles. We match each one to its registration.

  2. Forecasts and repeat faults, weeks 2 to 6

    Each Monday the list shows which vehicles are due in the next 30 days and which faults repeat.

  3. Mechanic-reviewed recommendations, every week

    Your mechanics approve, change or decline each recommendation, and the record keeps who decided.

Ask about a trial

What records do we need?

Job cards, service invoices and fault-code exports, as CSV, PDF or photos. Twelve months is enough to start.

Does it diagnose vehicles?

No. It points to patterns in your records; a qualified mechanic checks the vehicle and decides.

What does it cost?

A monthly subscription priced by the number of vehicles you manage. We quote after the trial.

Which vehicles does it support?

Vans, pickups and light trucks of any make, as long as you keep service records for them.

Can we stop the trial?

Yes, at any time. We delete your records and confirm it in writing.
