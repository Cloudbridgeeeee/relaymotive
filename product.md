# Product

RelayMotive: Intelligent insights for vehicle maintenance.

AI-powered vehicle maintenance software that analyzes service histories and diagnostic records to identify recurring maintenance problems and forecast servicing requirements.

- Founder and CEO: Chibuike Okafor
- Co-founder and CTO: Ridwan Adekunle
- Industry: Automotive, Machine Learning, Fleet Management
- Customers: Automotive workshops, fleet operators and vehicle leasing companies
- Core features: Maintenance forecasting, diagnostic record classification, recurring fault identification, service reminders, fleet maintenance analytics
- MVP: Vehicle service record uploads and mechanic-reviewed maintenance recommendations
- Planned AWS: SageMaker AI, Bedrock, S3, Lambda, DynamoDB
- Business model: Subscriptions based on managed vehicles
- Main action: Book a fleet trial
